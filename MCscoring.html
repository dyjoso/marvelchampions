Marvel Champions Scenario Scoring

This scoring system is inspired by, but totally unrelated to, the Solo Champions League scoring system developed by Innsmouth Bear.

Scoring Mode
Designer Mode

Game State Inputs

? Hover over the icon for a quick tip.

Hero + Ally HP: Sum of remaining HP for your hero and all allies.

Villain Total HP: Sum of printed HP for all villain stages (e.g., Rhino I + II).

Villain HP Left: For a loss, the villain's total remaining HP (if on stage I, add stage II hp).

Total Threat Pool: Sum of threat thresholds for all main scheme stages.

Threat Left: Sum of all threat on main and side schemes, plus total threat of any flipped main schemes.
